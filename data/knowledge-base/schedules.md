## Route Information

### Saigon → Da Lat
- Distance: ~300 km
- Highway:QL1A → National Route 20
- Rest stops: 1 stop at Bao Loc (30 minutes)

### Saigon → Nha Trang
- Distance: ~450 km
- Highway: QL1A
- Rest stops: 1 stop at Phan Rang (30 minutes)

### Saigon → Vung Tau
- Distance: ~120 km
- Highway: DT52
- No rest stops required
