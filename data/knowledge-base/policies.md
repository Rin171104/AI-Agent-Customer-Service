# Policies

## Cancellation Policy

| Time Before Departure | Refund Percentage |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| More than 24 hours | 80% |
| 12-24 hours | 50% |
| Less than 12 hours | 0% (No refund) |

### Special Cases

- **Medical emergency**: 100% refund with valid documentation
- **Accident**: 100% refund with police report
- **Company policy violation**: Case-by-case basis

## Booking Policy

- Maximum booking: 10 tickets per transaction
- Seat selection: First-come, first-served
- Seat hold timeout: 15 minutes
- Payment timeout: 24 hours after booking creation

## Luggage Policy

- Free luggage: 20kg per passenger
- Excess luggage: 10,000 VND per kg
- Prohibited items: Flammable materials, weapons, illegal substances
- Fragile items: Customer's responsibility

## Pickup/Dropoff Points

### Saigon
- Ben Xe Mien Dong (Eastern Bus Station)
- Quan 1 (District 1 Office)
- Quan Binh Thanh (Binh Thanh Office)

### Da Lat
- Ben Xe Da Lat (Da Lat Bus Station)
- Truong Canh (Truong Canh Office)

## Payment Methods

- Bank Transfer (Vietcombank, BIDV, VietinBank)
- MoMo E-wallet
- ZaloPay
- Cash at ticket office

## Bus Amenities

- Air conditioning
- Free WiFi (selected buses)
- Blankets and pillows (overnight routes)
- Water and snacks (selected routes)
